# Brandon Mann

I work at the intersection of organizational change, platform adoption, and hands-on product building. Through **Ship or Sink**, I build practical tools for coordinating work and bringing AI into everyday workflows.

My projects explore a question I care about: how do you make useful tools easier to adopt while keeping access, ownership, evidence, and operating costs understandable?

## How I approach the work

- Start with a concrete workflow and build something I can use.
- Use AI development tools to accelerate implementation, then inspect behavior and revise the product.
- Make boundaries explicit: a working prototype, a private integration, and a public product have different requirements.
- Document decisions and remaining gaps alongside features. Permission checks, reliable failure handling, and understandable setup are part of the product.

The repositories show projects at different stages. Their READMEs distinguish checked-in functionality, local work, and remaining release requirements. I do not treat a feature list as evidence of adoption or business impact.
